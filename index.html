ANDY'S LITTLE FREE LIBRARY
Our Story

It started with one little library.

A little boy in Louisa had a simple idea: put some books in a box, set it outside, and let his neighbors take them for free. No library card. No due dates. Just books, freely given.

That little boy is Andy. And that little wooden box became Andy's Little Free Library — a neighborhood landmark and a reminder that kids don't have to wait until they're grown up to make a difference.

Now that idea is growing into Andy's Impact: a project encouraging children everywhere to discover the joy of reading, giving, sharing, and making a real difference in their communities.
